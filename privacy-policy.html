Privacy Policy

How Infinity Maze handles local game data and first-party product analytics.

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Infinity Maze handles information when you use the app or visit this website. By using the app or website, you accept this policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact support: cameoshell09@gmail.com

User accounts

Infinity Maze does not require you to create an account or provide personal information.

Personal information

Infinity Maze does not collect personal information such as your name or email address. We do not collect keystrokes or other unrelated device data.

Data stored on device

Infinity Maze uses local storage for game settings and progress. This data stays offline on your device.

Analytics data

Starting with version 1.5.0, Infinity Maze sends first-party pseudonymous product events such as app open, maze start and completion, replay, daily challenge, control mode, paywall view, and purchase result. Data may include random installation and session identifiers, app and build version, operating system, locale, and fixed gameplay categories. Identifiers are HMAC-hashed before database storage. We do not store maze paths, Game Center identities, names, email addresses, free-form text, or raw IP addresses. Cloudflare provides the collection endpoint and database infrastructure and processes standard network information needed to deliver and protect the service. Analytics is used only to understand and improve the product, not for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents and guardians are encouraged to help children use apps and websites safely.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date on this page.

Your acceptance

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the app or website. Continuing to use the app or website means you accept the current policy.
