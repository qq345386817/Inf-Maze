How to play "Infinity Maze"

Learn the four play modes, fog, same-maze replay, platform controls, offline play and optional Pro benefits.

01

On iPhone or iPad

  • Tap toward the path, or use the direction buttons: tap for one step and hold to keep moving. Pinch to zoom and drag to pan.
  • Choose Rectangular, Hexagonal, Honeycomb or Circular from the maze type menu.
  • Hexagonal and Honeycomb mazes support six directions; tap diagonally to move along diagonal paths.
  • Tap Start Over to start a new game.
  • Use Easy, Medium, Hard, or Insane levels, worth 5, 15, 45, and 100 points.
  • Pro unlocks every hero and doubles lifetime leaderboard scores.
  • Use Print when you want a hard copy of the current maze.
  • Circular mazes support eight movement directions.
02

On Mac

  • Click toward a path or use the keyboard. Six-direction mazes use W/E/A/D/Z/X; circular mazes use Q/W/E/A/D/Z/X/C.
  • Choose Rectangular, Hexagonal, Honeycomb or Circular from the maze type menu.
  • Hexagonal and Honeycomb mazes support six directions; click or use supported controls toward the diagonal path.
  • Click Start Over to start a new game.
  • Use Easy, Medium, Hard, or Insane levels, worth 5, 15, 45, and 100 points.
  • Pro unlocks every hero and doubles lifetime leaderboard scores.
  • Use Print when you want a hard copy of the current maze.
  • Circular mazes support eight movement directions.
03

On Apple TV

  • Swipe on the Remote Clickpad to move in the nearest maze direction.
  • Hexagonal and Honeycomb mazes support six-direction movement by swiping diagonally.
  • The Remote arrow buttons are shortcuts for the main four directions.
  • Press Play/Pause on the Remote to open Settings.
  • Double tap the Clickpad on the Remote to open Game Center.
  • Use Easy, Medium, Hard, or Insane levels, worth 5, 15, 45, and 100 points.
  • Pro unlocks every hero and doubles lifetime leaderboard scores.
  • Circular mazes support eight movement directions.
  • Choose Rectangular, Hexagonal, Honeycomb or Circular from the maze type menu.

Four ways to find the exit.

Pick a play mode as well as a shape and difficulty. Mode rules change the challenge and how your run earns stars.

01

Classic

Explore and solve at your own pace.

02

Fewest Steps

Aim for an efficient route; stars give more weight to the shortest path.

03

Time Attack

Stars reward finishing within the target time.

04

No Backtrack

Reverse moves are blocked, so choose your route carefully.

Fog is an additional option: it limits how much of the maze you can see while you explore.

Before you start

Can I play offline?+

Yes. Core maze play works offline. Game Center features require a network connection and sign-in.

Can I replay the same maze?+

Yes. After finishing, review your time, steps, backtracks, stars and score. Play Again keeps the same layout; New Maze generates another one.

Is Infinity Maze free?+

The app is free to download and core maze play is available without Pro. Optional Pro purchases unlock every hero and double lifetime leaderboard scores. Check the app for current prices.

How do I move and explore?+

On iPhone and iPad, tap toward a path or tap and hold the direction buttons; pinch to zoom and drag to pan. On Mac, use the keyboard or click. On Apple TV, swipe the Siri Remote Clickpad.
